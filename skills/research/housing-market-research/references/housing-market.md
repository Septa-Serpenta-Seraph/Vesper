## Listing filter checklist (apply to every listing)
- [ ] Rooms / size (sq ft)
- [ ] Price within budget
- [ ] Heat type: heat pump / in-floor / boiler, or wood (primary wood = fail)
- [ ] Condition / age; newer or renovated preferred for in-floor/heat-pump
- [ ] Utilities: included or billed separately
- [ ] State (water/septic/electric) and who maintains
- [ ] Garage, yard, layout

## Square footage vs. garage
Listed `Sq Ft` is above-ground living area — bedrooms, living, kitchen, baths.
The garage is not counted; it appears separately as a two-car/detached note or
in a "total incl. garage" figure. Never add the garage to the living number when
comparing against a target size, and never assume a garage is included in rent.
Confirm attached vs. detached when parking matters.

## Wood-stove rule
A wood stove is acceptable ONLY as a backup. Any listing whose primary heat is wood gets a hard pass.

## Budget escalation — when 1,800 sq ft is forced, Augusta-grade
| Target sq ft | What it actually resolves to (Augusta/south of Augusta, 2026) | Expected rent | Split of ~$3,789/mo net |
|---|---|---|---|
| ~1,800 | 3BR or 4BR house | ~$2,000–2,800 | 53–74% — too high, not viable on a single income |
| ~1,100 | 2BR house | ~$1,550–1,700 | 41–45% |
| ~800–900 | 2BR apartment/condo | ~$1,200–1,550 | 32–41% |

Rule: if the room-size you asked for is only available on the 3BR side of the line, say so before filing a listing. A bigger house at >45% of net is a trade, not a free win.
