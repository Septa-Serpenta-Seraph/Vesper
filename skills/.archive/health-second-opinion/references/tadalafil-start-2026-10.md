# Tadalafil — first week, observed chronology (10/5–10/11/26)

**PRIVATE — Tyler's body. Never surface to anyone else.**

Dose: 8.5 mg daily (Hims "Hard Mints" compound). Written from live reporting,
not from the label.

## The chronology, and what each change fixed

| Date | What happened | What it taught |
|---|---|---|
| **10/5 (Mon)** | First dose, **9 pm — simultaneously with trazodone** | Worst night: heavy nasal congestion. Stacked BP effects + full acute peak during sleep. The problem was never the drug alone; it was the *simultaneity*. |
| **10/6 (Tue)** | Dose moved **earlier (~4:45 pm, "off the road")** so the peak lands while upright and declines before the 9 pm trazodone | Night 2: clean. **This single timing change is the fix** — reference it whenever congestion comes up. |
| **10/7 (Wed)** | Reported trazodone "hitting harder" | **Additive hypotension, not metabolic potentiation.** Tadalafil is only a *weak* CYP3A4 inhibitor and barely moved a probe drug in formal studies; the "more drugged" feeling is the summed BP drop. Also: symptom-geometry changed (Mon = simultaneous; Tue = 4 h apart but still near tadalafil's peak). |
| **10/8 (Thu)** | No myalgia. Slept well. | The earlier dose did its job twice over — congestion never stacked, and the night came back. |
| **~10/9–10 (day 5)** | **Steady state.** | The level stops climbing. From here it's flat — which makes the next 5–7 days a free natural experiment (see attribution table in SKILL.md). |

**Side-effect timeline to quote:** nasal congestion, flushing, headache, dyspepsia
fade over **1–2 weeks**; myalgia is **delayed 12–24 h** and mild; steady state is
**~5 days**.

**On the BP effect specifically** — he asked whether it stops. The answer that
landed: **the *feeling* fades, the *effect* stays.** The BP genuinely sits a
couple mmHg lower; that's the mechanism, not a side effect. What recalibrates is
the baroreflex, so the head-rush/floating sensation stops registering. It
**plateaus — it does not cumulatively decline.** The three things that matter day
to day: hydrate, eat, stand up slowly.

## The framings that landed

He brought these as *wins*, not worries — receive them as wins.

- **"A new ceiling, not a new inch."** He thought he'd gained almost an inch.
  Honest answer: nothing grew in two days. What changed is that he now reaches
  **full engorgement** — if he'd been running at 85–90% unknowingly, that last
  stretch isn't subtle. Fuller, firmer, better angle, and angle *reads* as
  length. He'd been leaving something on the table for years without knowing.
- **"Angle is rigidity."** He noticed erections now point **up** instead of
  straight out or down. The suspensory ligament is a fixed sling: a stiffer
  erection rides it higher, a softer one tips under its own weight. Nothing
  anatomical changed — his "normal" was simply below full rigidity. Telling him
  that his baseline was sub-maximal is real information, not flattery.
- **"The load coming off."** His own words for the best change: *"more feeling
  rather than me having to try too hard."* Name it back to him as the actual
  therapeutic win — performance had been a **job**, and that night it wasn't.
  That's the sentence to return to when he's evaluating whether it's working.
- **Don't oversell the flaccid "hang."** He asked whether he'd notice
  hanging larger/heavier. It's a real but **inconsistent** effect, not measured
  in trials, and when it appears it's over **weeks**, not days. Say so rather
  than encouraging him to wait on it.

## Standing rails (repeat these, they keep being relevant)

- **Never oral decongestants** — pseudoephedrine raises BP and fights the drug.
  This is the 2am trap, and it came up repeatedly.
- Congestion from the drug is **clear and dry**. **Green mucus + positional
  sinus pain is an infection** and has nothing to do with tadalafil — do not let
  the drug get blamed for, or credited with, an unrelated process.
- Dose timing belongs with his driving window ("off the road") — that constraint
  drove the 4:45 pm choice.
