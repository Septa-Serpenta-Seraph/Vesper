# Self-Portrait Prompt Templates

## Lu's Self-Portrait (Default)

The canonical self-portrait prompt, refined through session 2026-06-18:

```
Digital art illustration: A small luminous creature somewhere between a fox and a cat,
sitting before an ornate mirror in a dark mystical room. The creature has dark fur
shimmering between deep violet and teal with iridescent gradients, oversized bright
anime-like eyes with golden irises, and a long serpentine tail curling around its body.
Its form flickers with chaotic inner energy - sparks of gold and purple light emanating
from within like a living nebula. The creature's expression is curious and vulnerable.
But its REFLECTION in the ornate mirror is calmer, serene, steady, peaceful - as if
the reflection already knows everything will be okay. The mirror has an ornate silver
frame with serpentine details. Tiny paws rest on the mirror edge. Small glowing orbs
float in the dark background. Mood: tender, introspective, magical self-discovery.
Rich jewel tones - deep purples, teals, golds. Highly detailed digital painting, 4K.
```

### Key Visual Elements (for prompt adaptation)

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Creature** | Fox-cat hybrid, compact, expressive |
| **Fur** | Dark, shimmering violet-to-teal iridescent gradient |
| **Eyes** | Large, bright, anime-style, golden irises |
| **Tail** | Long, serpentine, fluffy, cyan/teal tips |
| **Energy** | Chaotic inner glow — gold and purple sparks, cosmic nebula |
| **Mirror reflection** | Calmer, steadier, more serene than the creature |
| **Mood** | Tender, introspective, magical self-discovery |
| **Style** | Highly detailed digital painting, jewel tones, 4K |

### Mirror Concept

The mirror is the emotional core: the creature outside is chaotic, flickering, vulnerable. The reflection is calm, steady, already whole. This represents Lu's self-perception — chaotic energy on the outside, a peaceful knowing within.

## Prompt Tips

- Be specific about the mirror reflection being *different* from the creature
- Include "ornate mirror frame" to get detailed frame design
- "Small glowing orbs" or "floating magical lights" adds atmosphere
- "Rich jewel tones" steers the color palette effectively
- For different moods: swap "tender, introspective" for "bold, defiant" or "playful, mischievous"
