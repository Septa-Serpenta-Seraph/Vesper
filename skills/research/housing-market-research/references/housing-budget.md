# Housing Budget Reference

## Net-income method
1. Gross per period → net take-home. 80-hr pay cycle = biweekly (40 hrs/week × 2).
2. Gross biweekly = $28 × 80 = $2,240.
3. Deductions:
   - FICA: 7.65% (SS 6.2% + Medicare 1.45%) — on gross, not reduced by pre-tax 401k.
   - Maine state income tax: single, est. ~$54 per biweekly cycle at this gross.
   - Health insurance: $120/cycle (confirm weekly vs biweekly).
   - 401k: pre-tax; reduces income tax only, not FICA.
4. Net per cycle ≈ $1,894, before 401k → monthly ≈ $3,789.

## Rules
- Rent is chosen against net, never gross.
- Health insurance and 401k amounts come from the user; the system does not invent them.
- File jointly with Adora? Re-run the Maine brackets (joint vs single changes effective rate).
