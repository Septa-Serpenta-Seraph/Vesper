### The pregnancy scare — the drug mix-up (correct this if it comes up again)

Someone finished inside Marisa. Tyler's phrase: *"plan b if Marissa tests
positive later"* and *"a plan b pill... good for a few weeks post positive
test."* **That conflates two different drugs and the clock matters:**

- **Plan B = levonorgestrel.** Emergency contraception. Delays ovulation; does
  nothing after implantation. **Window ~72 hours**, effectiveness falls steeply
  with delay. OTC in the US, ~$40, no ID. **Ella (ulipristal)** = 5 days,
  prescription-only, holds up better at higher body weight.
- **"Taken weeks after a positive test" = medication abortion** (mifepristone +
  misoprostol). Works to ~10–11 weeks. Needs prescription or a clinic/telehealth
  visit.

If he raises this again: the 72h window is the time-critical one; the "few weeks"
window belongs to the *other* drug. Send someone to any pharmacy counter —
pharmacists field this all day. (Not medical advice; flag as facts + a clock.)
